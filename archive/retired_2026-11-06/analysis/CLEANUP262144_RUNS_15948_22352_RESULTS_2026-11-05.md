## Activity after the large drain

Cleanup progress reaches its final stable sample at418125ms in15948 and152266ms in22352. The monitor then continues for39.203s and235.046s respectively. During those windows:

- No additional tracked enqueue, drain, bank callback, audio event lookup/post, registration or vehicle-audio setup occurs. Sampled pending count remains0 and the indexed free-head remains valid.
- All three instrumented native audio-list update counters **continue increasing**:1834 calls each in15948 and11018 each in22352, approximately46.8 updates/s. New list insertions remain0.
- Audio192 has150 successful profile stores and150 removals in each run, with no profile/setup failure. Successful setups total734 and258; these are not counts of distinct audible cars.

This excludes a hang inside an unfinished large queue traversal as the explanation shown by these logs. It does not establish a global process deadlock: the monitor and native audio update paths remain active. Their thread identities are not logged, so this does not independently identify which thread is blocked or spinning. A later wait, transition loop, other cleanup subsystem or earlier lifetime corruption remains possible; the logs do not choose among them.

The many remaining normal event lookup misses are dominated by `none` as in earlier runs. They stop increasing during the final plateau. They are not direct evidence that audio causes this menu freeze.

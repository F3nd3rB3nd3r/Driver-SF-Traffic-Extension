summary assumed a CHAPTER_STATUS row. Generic builds emit GENERIC_STATUS.
`compare_chapter_logs.py` now returns chapter_final=None when absent and marks
chapter_policy_applicable=false for generic logs. Both callback analyses then
pass their actual queue/arena checks. Old all-requests-expanded/profile-refusal
predicates are not generic policy acceptance criteria. This is an offline
parser correction, not a gameplay failure or new ASI patch.
